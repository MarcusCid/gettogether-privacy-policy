Child Safety Standards

GetTogether · Last Updated: October 27, 2026

These Child Safety Standards apply to the GetTogether mobile application ("GetTogether", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Marcus Cid. GetTogether is a social app that lets people create groups, share availability, and plan activities together. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and are committed to keeping children safe on our platform.

1. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following is strictly prohibited anywhere in GetTogether, including usernames, nicknames, profile pictures, group names and images, and activity details:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including any imagery, video, or text that depicts, encourages, or promotes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children
  • Sexualization of minors in any form
  • Child grooming, including befriending or contacting a child in order to facilitate sexual contact or the exchange of sexual imagery, whether online or offline
  • Sextortion, meaning threatening or blackmailing a child using real or alleged access to intimate images
  • Trafficking of children, including advertising or soliciting a child for commercial sexual exploitation
  • Inappropriate or predatory interaction targeted at a child
  • Using the App in any other way that endangers children

Anyone who creates, uploads, shares, or distributes such content, or uses the App to endanger a child, violates these standards and our terms, and will be removed from the App.

2. Age Requirement

GetTogether is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly allow children below the minimum age to hold an account. If we learn that an account belongs to a child below the minimum age, we will delete it.

3. Reporting Concerns

You can report content or users that you believe violate these standards directly inside the App using the in-app report/feedback feature. You can also block any user from within the App.

You may also contact us directly at marcusliencid@gmail.com. Please include as much detail as you can (for example, the username and group involved). Do not forward or attach the abusive material itself; describe where it can be found instead.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger or has been subject to abuse, exploitation, or trafficking, contact your local law enforcement right away. In Norway, contact the police (emergency number 112, or 02800 for non-emergencies).

4. How We Respond

  • We review every report of suspected CSAE or CSAM and take appropriate action.
  • Where we have actual knowledge of CSAM, we remove the content, suspend or delete the offending account, and preserve the information required by law.
  • We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant regional or national authority, including Norwegian law enforcement, in accordance with applicable law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement and child safety organizations and with Google Play on investigations.

5. Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including having a process in place to report confirmed CSAM to the relevant authorities. These standards should be read together with our Privacy Policy.
