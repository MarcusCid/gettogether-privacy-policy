# Privacy Policy for GetTogether **Last Updated: [date]** This Privacy Policy describes how GetTogether ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by [Company/Developer Name], collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. ## 1. Information We Collect ### 1.1 Account Information When you create an account, we collect: - Email address - Username and nickname - Profile picture (optional, if you choose to upload one) - Authentication data from Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, if you choose those methods (we receive only your name and email address from these providers; we do not receive your password) ### 1.2 Content You Provide - Group names and group profile images you create - Availability dates you mark in shared group calendars - Activity/event details you create within groups - Your responses (accept/decline) to group activities - Friend connections and blocked user lists you manage ### 1.3 Automatically Collected Information - Device push notification token (used to deliver notifications about group activity, invitations, and reminders) - Device identifier (used to manage notification delivery across your devices) - Timestamps of account creation and group activity ### 1.4 Permissions The App requests the following device permissions, only used for the stated purpose: - **Camera**: to take a profile picture - **Photo Library**: to select a profile picture from your device We do not access your camera or photo library except when you actively choose to update your profile picture. ## 2. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: - Create and manage your account - Enable you to create and join groups with other users - Display your availability and activity responses to members of your shared groups - Send push notifications about group invitations, activities, and updates - Enable a friends list and a blocking feature to manage who you interact with - Maintain and improve the App's functionality and security We do **not** use your data for third-party advertising, and we do **not** sell your personal information to third parties. ## 3. How Information Is Shared ### 3.1 With Other Users Because GetTogether is a group-coordination app, certain information is visible to members of groups you join: - Your username, nickname, and profile picture - Your marked availability dates within a shared group - Your acceptance/decline status on group activities This information is **not** visible to the general public — only to members of groups you belong to. ### 3.2 With Service Providers We use the following third-party services to operate the App. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations: | Service | Purpose | Data Involved | |---|---|---| | Firebase Authentication (Google) | Account creation and sign-in | Email, name, authentication tokens | | Firebase Realtime Database (Google) | Storing app data (profiles, groups, activities) | All account and content data listed above | | Firebase Storage (Google) | Hosting profile and group images | Uploaded images | | Firebase Cloud Functions (Google) | Backend logic for group management, notifications | Account and group data | | Google Sign-In | Optional sign-in method | Name, email | | Sign in with Apple | Optional sign-in method | Name, email (or Apple-provided private relay email) | | Expo Push Notification Service | Delivering push notifications | Device push token | ### 3.3 Legal Requirements We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal process, protect our rights, or protect the safety of users. ## 4. Data Retention and Deletion You may delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile settings). When you delete your account: - Your profile data is permanently removed from our database - Your profile image is removed from storage - You are removed from all groups you belong to - Groups that have no remaining members are deleted - Your authentication record is permanently deleted You may also request deletion of your data by contacting us at [contact email]. ## 5. To exercise these rights, contact us at [contact email]. ## 6.
