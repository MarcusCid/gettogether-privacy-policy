Privacy Policy for GetTogether

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GetTogether ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username and nickname
  • Profile picture (optional, if you choose to upload one)
  • Authentication data from Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, if you choose those methods (we receive only your name and email address from these providers; we do not receive your password)

1.2 Content You Provide

  • Group names and group profile images you create
  • Availability dates you mark in shared group calendars
  • Activity/event details you create within groups
  • Your responses (accept/decline) to group activities
  • Friend connections and blocked user lists you manage

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device push notification token (used to deliver notifications about group activity, invitations, and reminders)
  • Device identifier (used to manage notification delivery across your devices)
  • Timestamps of account creation and group activity

1.4 Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, only used for the stated purpose:

  • Camera: to take a profile picture
  • Photo Library: to select a profile picture from your device

We do not access your camera or photo library except when you actively choose to update your profile picture.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable you to create and join groups with other users
  • Display your availability and activity responses to members of your shared groups
  • Send push notifications about group invitations, activities, and updates
  • Enable a friends list and a blocking feature to manage who you interact with
  • Maintain and improve the App's functionality and security

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How Information Is Shared

3.1 With Other Users

Because GetTogether is a group-coordination app, certain information is visible to members of groups you join:

  • Your username, nickname, and profile picture
  • Your marked availability dates within a shared group
  • Your acceptance/decline status on group activities

This information is not visible to the general public — only to members of groups you belong to.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal process, protect our rights, or protect the safety of users.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile settings). When you delete your account:

  • Your profile data is permanently removed from our database
  • Your profile image is removed from storage
  • You are removed from all groups you belong to
  • Groups that have no remaining members are deleted
  • Your authentication record is permanently deleted

You may also request deletion of your data by contacting us at privacy@gettogetherapp.no.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (via your Profile settings)
  • Request deletion of your data (via in-app account deletion or by contacting us)
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g., disabling push notifications in Settings)
  • Opt out of specific notification types individually within the App

Residents of the European Economic Area (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA) may have additional rights. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@gettogetherapp.no.

6. Children's Privacy

GetTogether is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including Firebase's built-in authentication and database security rules, to protect your information. Data is transmitted using encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States, via Google Firebase infrastructure. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
